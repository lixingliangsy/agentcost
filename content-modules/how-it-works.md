# CostLens — How It Works

## Step 1: Ingest Spend Log
Paste or connect your agent's spend data — tokens, calls, and associated context (agent name, feature, user).

## Step 2: Attribute Cost
CostLens processes the data and breaks down spend by agent, feature, and user. See the top cost drivers at a glance.

## Step 3: Guardrail Report
Get a recommended per-agent budget cap and an early-warning rule to catch runaway loops before they drain your budget.

## Output
- Cost attribution breakdown by your chosen dimension
- Recommended budget guardrails per agent
- Early-warning alert configuration
- Audit-ready export
