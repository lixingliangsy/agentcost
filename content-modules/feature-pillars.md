# CostLens — Feature Pillars

## Pillar 1: Cost Attribution
See exactly where your money goes — by agent, by feature, by user. No more guessing which workflow is burning through your budget.

- Drill down to agent, feature, or user level
- Real-time token-to-dollar conversion
- Historical trends to spot cost creep

## Pillar 2: Budget Guardrails
Set per-agent budgets and get alerts before you hit them. Prevent runaway loops from draining your account.

- Per-agent budget caps
- Early-warning alerts at configurable thresholds
- Auto-pause options for critical budgets

## Pillar 3: Audit-Ready Ledger
Every spend event is logged with context. Export for finance or compliance reviews.

- Immutable audit trail
- copy as Markdown/JSON text for accounting systems
- Run-level traceability with timestamps
