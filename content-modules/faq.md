# CostLens — FAQ

## Q: How accurate is the cost attribution?
A: Accuracy depends on the quality of the spend data you provide. CostLens uses standard token-to-dollar conversion rates and attributes costs based on the context you supply (agent, feature, user).

## Q: Can I export data to my accounting system?
A: Yes, Pro and Enterprise plans include copy as Markdown/JSON text that works with most accounting systems.

## Q: What happens when I hit my budget limit?
A: On Pro and Enterprise, you'll receive an alert before reaching the limit. You can configure auto-pause for critical budgets.

## Q: Is my BYOK key secure?
A: Yes. BYOK keys are stored server-side only and never exposed to the browser. They are used only for cost calculation purposes.

## Q: Can I track costs across multiple LLM providers?
A: Yes, CostLens supports cost tracking for any LLM provider with token usage data.

## Q: What if I need higher run limits?
A: Contact us for Enterprise pricing with custom limits and features.

## Q: Do you offer team seats?
A: Enterprise plans include team collaboration features. Contact us for details.
